# Jarvis privacy policy

Jarvis is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own Windows PC. It's used only by its owner.

- **What it accesses:** when its owner connects a Google account, Jarvis can read and search Gmail,
  create drafts, send email when the owner approves each send, and read and edit Google Calendar events.
- **Where data goes:** Google data is fetched directly from Google to the owner's PC. Jarvis has no server
  of its own, and nothing is stored anywhere except on that PC. To answer a request, the relevant text is
  sent to Anthropic's Claude API, under Anthropic's commercial terms.
- **Storage:** sign-in tokens are kept in the owner's Jarvis data folder (%APPDATA%\Jarvis) and can be
  deleted at any time. Access can also be revoked at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- **Sharing:** none. Jarvis doesn't sell, share or use Google data for anything except answering the owner.

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy,
including the Limited Use requirements.
